Where tooltips go

Only where the visible UI hides something: an icon without a word, a short state with more behind it, or a control that is disabled. Labelled buttons without a shortcut get none, and nothing you need in order to act lives only in a tooltip. Shown after 500 ms, instantly while moving between neighbours.
