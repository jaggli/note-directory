Space, shape, motion

A 4 px grid, three control heights, four radii, three shadows, two durations. Dense like a code editor, never cramped: rows are 28 px, phones get 44 px targets.

Nested corners

A container hugging rounded controls takes outer radius = inner radius + padding, so both curves share a centre: the tool dock is 6 + 4 = 10, the zoom panel 6 + 3 = 9, menus 6 + 4 = 10, segmented controls 4 + 2 = 6. Containers with roomy padding (popovers, dialogs) keep their own radius.

Motion

90 ms for hover and press, 160 ms for anything that appears: menus fade and drop 4 px, the palette and dialogs fade and scale from 98 %, toasts rise 8 px. One curve, cubic-bezier(.2, 0, 0, 1). Exits are instant.

Never animated: typing, the caret, scrolling, dragging, zoom, switching notes, Edit ⇄ Preview. Under prefers-reduced-motion everything is instant except the sync pulse, which becomes a static dot.
