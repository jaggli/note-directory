Sidebar and rows

The sidebar answers “what notes do I have, and are they safe”. Header: the app menu and New. Then Search, the list, and a footer with sync and storage for all notes.

The sidebar

  • note ▾ is the app menu. The wordmark shrinks from 22 px to 15 px but stays in Victor Mono.
  • Search looks like a field and opens the palette — the same thing ⌘P does.
  • Pinned gets a label once something is pinned; without pins there are no sections.
  • Hover swaps the time for Pin and ⋯. On touch they're behind long-press.
  • Rename in place: Enter commits, Esc cancels, a taken name is refused inline.
  • Footer: sync state for all notes, and storage once it passes half the quota (today's threshold).
