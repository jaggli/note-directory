Typography

Two faces with two jobs. The platform face (SF Pro, Segoe UI) for chrome: it is what every app on the machine uses for buttons, so ours read as buttons. Victor Mono for what you type and what you'd type: the note, code, keys, and the wordmark. Sentence case, no all-lowercase, no all-caps.

Rules

  • Sentence case for every label, title, toast and tooltip: “Export all as ZIP”, not “export all notes” or “Export All Notes”.
  • No periods in labels, toasts or tooltips. Full sentences in dialogs and banners get them.
  • Extensions are muted, never dropped. release-notes.md — the extension decides how a note behaves, so it stays visible but quiet.
  • Tabular figures for times, sizes, counts and positions so the status bar doesn't wobble while you type.
  • Keys in the platform's glyphs: ⌘ ⌥ ⇧ ⌃ on Mac, “Ctrl+Shift+P” elsewhere. Never “ctrl+” on a Mac.
